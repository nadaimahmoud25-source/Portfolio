How I take a project from unclear to shipped

From a messy first brief to a product that’s ready to ship, with AI and code in the loop.

01 / Unlock ambiguity

Turn an unclear brief into a clear problem

I clear up ambiguity early and keep PMs, stakeholders and engineers aligned at every step.

02 / Prototype with AI

Prototype in code, not only in Figma

I build working prototypes with AI, on top of the existing codebase when there is one, so we test real flows with real data in days, not weeks. The design judgement stays mine.

03 / Align

Decide by using, not imagining

Business, product and engineering try the prototype themselves. I walk through the trade-offs and bring everyone to one decision.

04 / Ship

Ship from a head start

Because the prototype lives in the codebase, engineering starts from working code. I design every rule, language and edge case, and stay close until it’s live.
